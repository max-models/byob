byob builds a static plasma background for tracking test particles: the electron and ion
densities, the temperatures, the potential and the electric field at any position, in 1D, 2D or
3D. Nothing is solved self-consistently. The bulk is interpolated from a few probe values over a
uniform grid and its potential follows Boltzmann's relation; the Debye sheath and presheath in
front of every wall are added analytically at the evaluation point, so they stay sharp however
coarse the grid.

Each equation on these pages is declared on the code that implements it, as a method card, and
checked by the tests: the closed forms of the sheath, the presheath and the Boltzmann potential
against SymPy rules, the probe fill, the gather and the bulk field against their order of
convergence. The pages of the section list the cards module by module; this one shows how they
fit together.

## From probes to the wall

1. **[Probe interpolation](/byob/numerics/probes/).** Each of $n$, $T_e$ and $T_i$ is fixed at
   the nodes nearest its probes and spread over the other nodes by a harmonic or (by default)
   biharmonic equation on the grid graph, in $\ln Q$ so it stays positive. Walls and the domain
   boundary are zero-gradient, so values flow around electrodes instead of through them:

<MethodCard name="byob.probes.fill_probes" headingLevel={3} />

2. **[Boltzmann potential](/byob/numerics/background/).** `Background.build` sets the bulk
   potential on the nodes from $n$ and $T_e$, anchored at a `potential` probe or at the density
   maximum.
3. **[Sheath and presheath](/byob/numerics/sheath/).** At a point at distance $d$ from a wall,
   the bulk state of the point gives the presheath drop $T_e/2$ to the Bohm speed, the sheath
   drop of a floating or biased wall, and the Child-Langmuir width; the potential and the
   densities follow in closed form:

<MethodCard name="byob.sheath.wall_layer" headingLevel={3} />

4. **[Evaluation](/byob/numerics/background/).** `Background.evaluate` gathers the bulk
   linearly from the grid and replaces $n_e$, $n_i$ and $\phi$ by the layer of the controlling
   wall, the one of lowest potential; `Background.electric_field` adds $-\nabla\phi_\mathrm{bulk}$
   from the grid and $-(d\phi/dd)\,\nabla d$ from the layer.

## Units and conventions

Positions in m, densities in m$^{-3}$, temperatures in eV (so $T/e$ is written $T$), potentials
in V and fields in V/m. A wall is a simgeometry shape with a signed distance $d$, negative
inside; positions are `(N, ndim)`. The ion mass defaults to the proton mass.

## Not in the model

- No Poisson equation: the sheath potential (Child-Langmuir, ion space charge only) and the
  sheath densities (Boltzmann electrons, Bohm ions) are not mutually consistent.
- No electron sheaths: a wall biased above the sheath edge potential gets the presheath only.
  No wall currents and no secondary electron emission.
- The layer uses the bulk state at the evaluation point, taken as constant across it: gradients
  of $n$ and $T_e$ along the layer do not enter $\mathbf E$.
- The $4/3$ power of the sheath potential is taken about the wall, not about the sheath edge:
  the field is zero at the wall and largest at the edge, the reverse of the Child-Langmuir
  solution. The width, the drop, the end values and the densities as functions of $\phi$ are
  unaffected.
- Temperatures are unchanged in the sheath and presheath; $T_i$ enters only the floating drop.
- No magnetic field, no time dependence, no collisions (those are plasmacoll's, fed by
  `Background.profile`).
- Outside the grid the bulk is constant (the edge value); the walls are evaluated exactly
  anywhere.

## Verification

Besides the checks of the cards, the tests check that the harmonic fill does not overshoot its
probes, that the biharmonic fill has no cusp at a probe, that a region without a probe is NaN,
and, on a uniform plasma in front of a floating wall, the wall potential and that the field is
$-\nabla\phi$ by finite differences of `evaluate`. The
[biased electrode](/byob/example-scripts/03-biased-electrode/) shows the sheath widening as
$\Delta\phi^{3/4}$, and [tracking ions](/byob/example-scripts/11-tracking-ions/) ions reaching
the sheath edge near the Bohm speed.
