## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Probes" icon="pencil">
		Density, temperatures and potential at a few points, interpolated smoothly over a grid and around walls.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Sheaths" icon="warning">
		Floating and biased walls get a Debye sheath and presheath from the local electron temperature, evaluated analytically so they stay sharp on coarse grids.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Particle tracking" icon="rocket">
		Densities, temperatures, potential and electric field at particle positions, and plasmacoll profiles.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Plotting" icon="seti:image">
		`Background.sample` returns xarray data for plasma-plots: maps, lineouts and field arrows.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
